Your Tradeprint Pro

Know your best setup, day and time window.

Every strategy ranked by P&L, win rate, sample size and rule discipline, plus the confluences that hold up and the ones that cost you when they're missing.

  • Clarity score for how complete your logs are
  • Best and worst trading days and time windows
  • Breakdowns by market, direction and hold time

Journal and calendar Free

Every trade, with the context that matters.

Log the strategy, confluences, setup grade, mistakes and whether you followed your rules. Then open any day on the calendar to see its trades, news and notes.

  • Rule breaks flagged automatically
  • A playbook for every strategy
  • Chart screenshots and daily notes

Prop Firm Command Center Pro

Every prop firm account. One command center.

Link each evaluation or funded account to your journal. Tradeprint tracks profit targets, drawdown, consistency and payouts against that account's rules, and what you actually made after costs.

Rule templates for Topstep, Apex Trader Funding, Lucid Trading, Tradeify, My Funded Futures, FundedNext Futures, Take Profit Trader and Alpha Futures, or your own custom rules.

Economic news Free

Know what's on the calendar before you trade.

High-impact U.S. releases from the BLS, BEA, Census Bureau, Federal Reserve and Department of Labor, on your calendar with actual and previous figures.

Mentorship Pro

Get your trades reviewed by someone you trust.

Invite a mentor by email, choose exactly what they can see, and request reviews on a trade or a whole day. Mentors join free.

Prints Pro

Share trades with people who journal too.

Post a logged trade with or without the P&L, show the confluences behind it, follow traders and reply. Leaderboards are opt-in.

Calculator Pro

Know your risk before you enter.

Futures P&L, points and position size calculators, with contract specs for MNQ, NQ, MES, ES, MGC and GC.

  • Share cards Free

  • Trading rules Free

    Checks for time windows, trade counts, daily loss and weekdays.

  • Every market

    Shares, futures, options, forex and crypto in one journal.

  • Multiple accounts
