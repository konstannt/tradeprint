Your Tradeprint Pro

Know your best setup, day and time window.

Tradeprint ranks every strategy by P&L, win rate, sample size and rule discipline, then shows which confluences hold up and which ones cost you when they're missing.

  • Clarity score tells you how complete your trade logs are.
  • Best and worst trading days and time windows.
  • Breakdowns by market, long or short, and hold time.

Journal and calendar Free

Every trade, with the context that matters.

Log the strategy, confluences, setup grade, mistakes and whether you followed your rules. The calendar turns all of it into a month you can actually review.

  • Rules checked for you

    Set limits like a max number of trades, a daily loss in dollars or R, trading hours and weekdays. Tradeprint flags breaks on the calendar and while you log.

  • A playbook for every strategy

    Entry criteria, invalidation, targets, risk notes and the confluences you check off on each trade.

  • Charts and notes in one place

    Attach chart screenshots to trades and keep daily notes, so your review has everything it needs.

Prop Firm Command Center Pro

Every prop firm account. One command center.

Link each evaluation or funded account to your journal. Tradeprint tracks profit targets, drawdown, daily loss, consistency and payouts against the rules saved for that account, then shows what you actually made after costs.

Rule templates for popular futures prop firms, including Topstep, Apex Trader Funding, Lucid Trading, Tradeify, My Funded Futures, FundedNext Futures, Take Profit Trader and Alpha Futures, or set up your own custom rules.

Prop firm numbers are tracking tools based on your Tradeprint data and the rules saved for each account. Firm rules change, so always confirm eligibility with your firm. Tradeprint is not affiliated with any prop firm.

Economic news Free

Know what's on the calendar before you trade.

High-impact U.S. releases from the BLS, BEA, Census Bureau, Federal Reserve and Department of Labor sit right on your P&L calendar, with actual and previous figures once they're out.

Mentorship Pro

Prints Pro

Post a logged trade to Prints with or without the P&L, show the confluences behind it, follow other traders and reply. Leaderboards are opt-in.

Why Tradeprint

  • One login instead of five tabs.

    Journal, analytics, prop tracking, economic news and mentor reviews work from the same trade log, so nothing gets copied between spreadsheets.

  • The process, not just the P&L.

    Rules, setup grades and mistakes are logged with every trade, so a lucky win and a well-executed trade never look the same.

  • Built on your own data.

    No signals and no predictions. Tradeprint only shows patterns from the trades you log, so you decide what to change.
