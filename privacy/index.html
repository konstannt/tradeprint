1. Introduction

Tradeprint is operated by Konstantinos Tsiomplikas, a sole proprietor in the Commonwealth of Massachusetts (“Tradeprint,” “we,” “us,” or “our”).

This Privacy Policy explains how Tradeprint collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information when you use tradeprint.ai and related Tradeprint services.

By using Tradeprint, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information.

Account information. This may include your name, email address, account identifier, authentication information, profile information, and account settings.

Trading and journal information. This may include trades you enter or import, instruments, profit and loss information, strategies, trading rules, mistakes, notes, screenshots, journal entries, prop-firm information, and other information you choose to record.

Subscription and transaction information. We may receive information regarding your Tradeprint subscription, subscription status, invoices, discounts, promotion codes, payment status, Stripe customer identifiers, and related transaction records.

We do not generally receive or store your complete payment-card number when payments are processed by Stripe.

Creator and affiliate information. If you participate in the Creator Program, we may collect or maintain your Creator profile, referral codes, attributed subscriptions, commission rates, commission history, refund reversals, payout eligibility, payout history, and related records.

Stripe Connect information. If you set up creator payouts, Stripe may collect identity, business, tax, banking, and verification information directly from you. Tradeprint may receive connected-account identifiers, verification and onboarding status, payout capability status, limited account information, transfer information, payout information, and other data Stripe makes available to Tradeprint through Connect.

Mentorship information. We may process mentor relationships, invitations, permissions, trading information shared with mentors, feedback, communications, and related activity.

Communications. We may collect information you send to us through support requests, email, forms, surveys, or other communications.

Device and usage information. We may collect IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, timestamps, referring information, application events, error logs, security events, and similar technical information.

Cookies and local storage. Tradeprint may use cookies, browser storage, or similar technologies for authentication, preferences, security, and application functionality.

3. How We Use Information

We may use personal information to provide and maintain Tradeprint; authenticate users; provide subscriptions and Pro features; store and analyze trading journals; provide mentorship functionality; calculate Creator attribution and commissions; detect refunds and commission reversals; administer Stripe Connect onboarding and payouts; process payments; provide customer support; maintain security; detect fraud and abuse; troubleshoot errors; improve our products; communicate service-related information; enforce our agreements; and comply with legal, tax, accounting, and regulatory obligations.

We may also use aggregated or de-identified information for analytics, product development, and business purposes where permitted by law.

4. Payments and Stripe

Tradeprint uses Stripe for certain payment, subscription, refund, and payout-related services.

When you make a payment, Stripe may collect payment information directly from you.

Creators using Stripe Connect may also provide Stripe with identity, tax, business, or banking information.

Stripe processes information according to its own agreements and privacy practices.

Where permitted by Stripe Connect and applicable law, Tradeprint may receive and use Connected Account data to administer Creator onboarding, eligibility, transfers, payouts, refunds, reconciliation, fraud prevention, and compliance.

Tradeprint does not use Connected Account information for purposes unrelated to operating the Tradeprint Creator Program.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information for money.

We may disclose information to service providers that help us operate Tradeprint, such as payment processors, cloud hosting providers, databases, authentication providers, email providers, analytics or monitoring providers, and customer-support providers.

We may share information with Stripe where necessary to process subscriptions, facilitate Stripe Connect, perform transfers or payouts, verify connected accounts, prevent fraud, and comply with financial requirements.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, court order, subpoena, regulatory obligation, tax requirement, or valid legal process; to investigate fraud, abuse, security threats, or violations of our Terms; to protect Tradeprint, our users, or others; or in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of all or part of our business.

Mentorship information is shared only according to features and permissions chosen by the relevant users.

We do not intentionally make private trading journal information publicly available unless a user chooses to share it through a Tradeprint sharing feature.

6. Creator Information and Stripe Connect

Creators who participate in payouts may be required to establish a Stripe connected account.

Tradeprint may create or connect that account, request onboarding links, determine whether onboarding requirements are complete, view payout-related account status, initiate authorized transfers, reconcile Creator earnings, and receive transfer or payout status information.

Stripe may independently request additional information from Creators to comply with financial or regulatory requirements.

Creators should review information presented during Stripe onboarding before submitting it.

7. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Tradeprint may use necessary cookies and browser storage to maintain sessions, authenticate users, remember preferences, prevent abuse, and support application functionality.

If Tradeprint later uses non-essential advertising, behavioral tracking, or similar technologies requiring additional consent, we may provide additional notices or controls as required by applicable law.

8. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, maintain legitimate business records, comply with legal and tax obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, prevent fraud, and maintain financial and Creator payout records.

Different categories of information may be retained for different periods.

Financial, commission, payout, refund, and transaction records may need to be retained after an account is closed.

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards intended to protect personal information.

No internet service or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your own credentials and notifying us if you believe your account has been compromised.

10. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights relating to your personal information, such as the right to request access, correction, deletion, portability, or information regarding our processing of your data.

Certain information may not be deleted immediately where retention is necessary for legal obligations, fraud prevention, accounting, payment records, Creator commission records, disputes, or security.

You may contact us using the information below to submit a privacy request.

We may need to verify your identity before completing certain requests.

11. Marketing Communications

If Tradeprint sends optional promotional emails, you may unsubscribe using the mechanism provided in those communications.

Service, security, billing, payout, and account-related messages may still be sent when necessary to operate your account.

12. Children

Tradeprint is intended for users who are at least 18 years old.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child in violation of applicable law, we will take appropriate steps to delete or otherwise address that information.

13. International Users

Tradeprint and its service providers may process or store information in countries other than your country of residence, including the United States.

Where required, appropriate safeguards may be used for international transfers of personal information.

14. Third-Party Services and Links

Tradeprint may contain links to or integrations with third-party websites and services.

This Privacy Policy does not govern the independent privacy practices of third parties.

You should review their privacy policies before providing information directly to them.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Tradeprint evolves or legal requirements change.

The “Last Updated” date will indicate the most recent revision.

For material changes, we may provide additional notice through Tradeprint, email, or another reasonable method.
