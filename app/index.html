Journal

Log every trade with the context that actually matters.

A trade journal is only as good as what goes into it. Tradeprint gives every trade a home for the details a spreadsheet loses, so your trade tracking is still useful weeks later when you sit down to review.

  • Strategy and confluences. Pick the setup and check off which confluences were actually there.
  • The numbers. Result, P&L, R:R and entry time, plus entry and exit price, size, risk and partial exits when you want the full detail.
  • Execution. A setup grade, mistake tags like FOMO or a moved stop, and whether you followed your rules.
  • Charts and notes. Attach chart screenshots and write down what happened while it is still fresh.
  • Multiple accounts. Keep broker and prop accounts separate, or look at all of them together.

Improve

Track the process, not just the P&L.

Trading discipline is easier to keep when it is measured. Tradeprint tracks the decisions behind each trade, so a lucky win and a well-executed trade never look the same.

Rules checked automatically

Set rules like no trades after a certain time, a maximum number of trades or losing trades per day, a daily loss limit in dollars or R, or the weekdays you trade. Tradeprint flags violations on the calendar and warns you while you log a trade.

Mistakes and setup quality

Tag mistakes, grade every setup and see where your execution slips.

Playbooks and notes

Write entry criteria, invalidation, targets and risk notes for each strategy, save setup examples, and keep daily notes with charts and linked trades.

Prop trading

A prop firm trading journal that keeps up with the rules.

The Prop Firm Command Center links each evaluation or funded account to the trades you already log, then tracks it against the rules saved for that account.

  • Evaluation progress. Profit target, consistency and minimum trading days, with a clear next objective.
  • Risk room. Drawdown and daily loss remaining. Intraday trailing drawdown is estimated from your closed trades.
  • Payouts. Qualifying days, buffers, caps and a full payout history for every account.
  • True prop profitability. Account costs, resets and fees against the payouts you actually received.
  • Rule templates. Start from templates for popular futures prop firms, or set up your own rules.

Prop Firm Command Center is part of Tradeprint Pro. Its numbers are tracking tools based on your Tradeprint data and the rules saved for each account. Firm rules change, so always confirm eligibility and current rules with your prop firm.
