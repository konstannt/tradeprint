How can we help?

If you need help with your account, have a billing question, or found a bug, reach out and we'll help you sort it out.

Contact Support

Support is handled over email. Send us a message and we'll help you out.

Email tradeprint.ai@gmail.com
Email Support

When you email us, include the email address on your Tradeprint account and a short description of the issue. Please don't send passwords, full payment card numbers, or other sensitive credentials.

We can help with

  • Account or login issues
  • Tradeprint Pro and billing
  • Subscription or refund questions
  • Creator program or payout questions
  • Mentorship questions
  • Bugs or technical issues
  • Feature questions or feedback
